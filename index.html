About

I’m a CSE student at Vellore Institute of Technology, just starting my degree. I like figuring out how things work, then building small projects to prove I understand them.

I learn best by building. I read the docs, break things, ask questions, and write down what I figured out so I can come back to it later. That habit has already taken me from classroom exercises to a hackathon project and a club project.

Right now I’m getting comfortable with programming fundamentals and exploring areas like web development, data structures and AI. I’m looking for teammates, clubs and hackathons where I can learn from people ahead of me, and I’m happy to help others when I can.
